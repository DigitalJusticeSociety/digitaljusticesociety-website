Welcome to the **Digital Justice Society**! We're a group of people dedicated to bringing just, ethical and sustainable technology to our communities. We believe decentralised technology has the power to liberate people and groups to organise in ways that benefit their own interests.

This website exists features our blog, overviews of our activities and workshops, and downloads of our published resources :).
